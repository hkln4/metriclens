Details

Limitations

  • Leakage screening covers single-feature association and numeric correlation only. Leakage through combinations of features, through time, through preprocessing, or through repeated entities is not detected; a clean screen is not proof of no leakage.
  • Thresholds are rule triggers, not verdicts. Each finding shows its rule version and thresholds; values whose interval straddles a threshold are marked 'near threshold'.
  • Column kinds and the task are inferred heuristically; check the schema table.
  • Experiments test one change at a time against the same base setup; interactions between changes are not measured.
  • Experiment results are development evidence from cross-validation on the dev rows. A claim about generalization needs the untouched holdout (or nested CV when there is none).
